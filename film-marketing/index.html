03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Is this only for theatrical releases, or also for streaming/OTT?

Covers both — a theatrical campaign and an OTT-first release need different marketing approaches, and we scope accordingly.

Do you handle trailer cutting as part of this, or just the campaign around it?

We handle clip-farming — finding and cutting the right clips to promote the film — which needs proper planning, time and coordination with the creative team.

How far in advance of release should we start?

At least 6–8 weeks before a theatrical release.

Do you manage paid social/digital spend for the campaign, or only creative/strategy?

We specialise in running paid campaigns on Meta, Google and Amazon as part of film marketing — this page covers film-specific strategy and creative, with Digital Marketing handling broader paid campaign execution where needed.
