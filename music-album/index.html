03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you work with independent artists or only labels?

Either — we work with anyone making good music who wants a visual for it, independent artists and labels alike.

Do you handle locations, styling and choreography coordination?

Yes — production support beyond the camera (locations, styling/art direction, coordinating choreography or dancers where needed) is part of the process.

Can this be released alongside a broader marketing push?

Yes — see Film Marketing and OTT Distribution if the video is part of a wider release or campaign. We also run awareness and reach campaigns directly on Meta, Google and Amazon.

What's a realistic timeline from concept to final cut?

3–4 weeks at minimum, from concept through to post-production.

Should the video be built around the artist performing, or around the song's concept?

Either, depending on the song — an artist-as-hero video is built around performance and emotion; a concept-driven video is built around the lyrics or theme. Either way, we bring full film-production capability to it so it feels like a complete film.
