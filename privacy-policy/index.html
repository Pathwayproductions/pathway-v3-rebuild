Privacy & Cookie Policy
.

How we collect, use and protect information when you visit pathwayproductions.in, and how we use cookies for analytics and advertising.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

Pathway Productions ("Pathway", "we", "us") is a creative production and digital marketing company based in Chennai, India, with a presence in Bengaluru. This policy covers the website at pathwayproductions.in and explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have — including about cookies.

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email address, phone number, company and any message details, when you submit a contact or enquiry form, or write to us directly.
  • Information collected automatically — through cookies and similar technologies, such as pages visited, how you arrived at the site, device and browser type, and general location (city/country level). This category only becomes active once you consent to it, as described below.

Cookies and how we use them

When you first visit the site, a banner asks you to Accept all or Reject non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored on your device and applied automatically on later visits. You can change it at any time — see "Managing your preferences" below.

Essential cookies — always on

These are required for the site to function (for example, remembering your cookie choice) and do not require consent.

Analytics cookies — only with your consent

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, time on site, scroll depth, outbound link clicks and file downloads — so we can improve it. This data is aggregated and does not include your name or contact details unless you've submitted a form.

Advertising cookies — only with your consent

If you accept, we may use Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and LinkedIn tags to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and, where enabled, to show you relevant ads on those platforms. These are configured through Google Tag Manager and only load after you've granted consent.

We manage all of the above through Google Tag Manager (container ID GTM-NPQF6K8) and Google Consent Mode v2, which signals your consent choice to Google's tools directly, so nothing analytics- or advertising-related runs before you've made a choice.

Managing your preferences

You can change your cookie choice at any time by clicking the button below, or by clearing your browser's site data for pathwayproductions.in, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our contact forms
  • Understand how people use our site and improve it
  • Measure and optimize our marketing campaigns, where you've consented to advertising cookies
  • Meet legal and contractual obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties we share data with

Where you've consented, data may be shared with Google (Analytics, Ads, Tag Manager), Meta and LinkedIn, solely for the analytics and advertising purposes described above, governed by each provider's own privacy policy.

Data retention

We keep enquiry/contact form submissions for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business record, and analytics data for the retention period set in our Google Analytics configuration (typically up to 14 months).

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@pathwayproductions.in. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our tools or practices change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@pathwayproductions.in.
