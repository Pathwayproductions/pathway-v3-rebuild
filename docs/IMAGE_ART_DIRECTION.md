# Image creation pipeline — ChatGPT → Claude

ChatGPT owns concept development, art direction and generation of **suggested** images. User approves images. Claude integrates approved exports, responsive crops, alt text and attribution/licensing metadata.

## Required asset record
Each image must have: `asset_id`, `target_page`, `target_section`, `visual_goal`, `source_type`, `source_file`, `desktop_crop`, `mobile_crop`, `focal_point`, `alt_text`, `approval_status`.

## Visual standards
- Naturalistic, believable, positive, cinematic but not overly stylised.
- Large real-world environments and authentic interactions, not repeated close-ups of camera hardware.
- Wide landscape hero with safe negative space for typography; avoid putting typography directly inside generated bitmap unless explicitly approved.
- Distinct composition for each service and section; no repeated hero or generic stock motifs.
- Source/master image separately from final compressed variants; do not bake in buttons, brand text or UI.

## Initial image priority
1. Creative Productions: wide, daylight real film set with substantial active crew, authentic location and layered production activity; negative space for editorial heading.
2. Corporate Films: modern working environment with authentic filming interaction, not anonymous skyscraper stock.
3. Crew & Equipment: large professional crew coordinating location production, with believable rigs and people working rather than posing.
4. Amazon Advertising: relevant ecommerce product/creative strategy context; avoid trademark misrepresentation or fake performance data.
5. Marketing Intelligence: real strategy collaboration and grounded data review, not fictional analytics metrics.
6. Contact: warm, approachable studio/office environment, uncluttered and welcoming.

No image is approved by its presence in the prototype. Approval is explicit and asset-specific.
