04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you buy the airtime, or only prepare the film for broadcast?

We handle airtime buying as well, not just preparing the film for broadcast. Payment for airtime is fully in advance, and the cost is quoted based on the specific programme slot and channel you want the commercial to run on.

What format/technical specs does a TVC need to meet for broadcast?

Typically 4K; in some cases Full HD or 2K, depending on the channel's requirements.

Can you help us decide which channels/time slots to target?

Yes — we suggest channels and slots based on your target audience, ad concept and business goals.

Do you handle regional-language versions for regional broadcast?

Currently we work with Tamil satellite channels only.
