02 / Why This Matters

What the Right Video Actually Does for a Business.

91% of businesses now use video as a core part of their marketing.
85% of people say a video has convinced them to buy a product or service.
89% say the quality of a brand's video shapes how much they trust it.

Numbers like these are why most businesses now treat video as essential, not optional. But they also point to the harder part: quality and trust aren't things a vendor can deliver from a brief alone. The films that actually move a business forward are usually made by a team that takes the time to understand the business behind them — its goals, its tone, the way its founder or leadership actually talks about the work — before a camera is ever switched on. That's the difference between a video that looks good and one that does something for the brand.

Source: Wyzowl, State of Video Marketing 2026

06 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

How long does a typical video take to produce?

Timeline depends on format and scope — a single ad film usually moves faster than a multi-location corporate documentary or a fully animated explainer. We map out a clear, dated schedule as soon as we understand your brief, so there are no surprises mid-project.

What's included — do you handle the whole process, or just the shoot?

End to end. Concept and scripting, storyboarding where needed, the shoot itself (crew, location, equipment), and post-production — edit, colour, sound and motion graphics. One team, one point of contact, start to finish.

Do you only work with film and entertainment brands, or also corporates and institutions?

Mostly the opposite. Ad films, corporate films, explainer videos and animation for businesses and institutions across manufacturing, logistics, education, FMCG, BFSI and more — see a few of them on our Work page.

We already have brand guidelines — can you work within them?

Yes. If you have an existing brand guideline, tone of voice or visual identity, we build the film around it. If you don't yet, a short discovery conversation gets us there before any scripting starts.

What does a project typically cost?

It depends on format, length, number of shoot days, locations and whether animation or VFX is involved — a 60-second ad film and a multi-location documentary aren't priced the same way. Share your brief and we'll give you a clear, itemised estimate before anything is confirmed.
