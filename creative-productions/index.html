06 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Getting Started
Do you only work with film and entertainment brands, or also corporates and institutions?

Mostly the opposite — ad films, corporate films, explainer videos and animation for businesses and institutions across manufacturing, logistics, education, FMCG, BFSI and more.

We already have a concept or script — can you work with it?

Yes — we can work with an approved script from your side. We've also worked on projects where a client's script evolved into a better version after mutual discussion. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you build one through a short discovery conversation.

What do you need from us to get started?

More than a video brief — tell us about your company, product, market, target audience and the problem you're trying to solve, not just what you imagine the video should look like. We usually start with a call or an in-person meeting, then ask for more resources — documents, existing material, access to your people — and often a site visit or product demonstration before we start writing anything.

Process & Collaboration
What does the script and pre-production stage actually involve?

More than just a voiceover script — we build out the likely visuals, animation or transition approach and storyboards alongside it, so you have a clear sense of how the final film will look before we shoot or animate anything.

What's the biggest thing that slows a project down on the client side?

Saving up corrections for the final cut instead of flagging them stage by stage — storyboard, script, rough cut. The more responsive and specific the feedback at each stage, the less rework later.

How do we actually give feedback on a cut?

Through Frame.io, mainly — we share the video on a link where you (and anyone else on your side) can drop time-stamped comments directly on the frame you're referring to. We're happy to use WhatsApp or email too if that's what works for your team.

Should this be one video, or several shorter ones?

Depends on what you're trying to communicate. If you have several distinct products, features or messages, we often recommend a short series — one video per feature or audience — rather than cramming everything into one.

Does the video need to be short — under 90 seconds or 2 minutes?

Not necessarily — that's a common assumption, not a rule. Duration should follow your audience and platform, not a fixed number. Where it's useful, we'll recommend a few duration cuts for different platforms rather than forcing one version everywhere.

Team & Capability
You offer both creative production and digital marketing — which one are you stronger at?

We run two dedicated teams, each built for its own discipline. Both are strong, but the real advantage is that each one feeds the other — the data and targeting parameters from digital marketing inform creative decisions on a video, and creative insight in turn shapes how we approach a digital campaign.

How big is your team — can a small studio really handle a project this size?

We run a focused in-house team of around 10–15 people — creative directors, video producers, editors, digital campaign specialists, digital marketing managers, website developers and client servicing — plus an extended network of vetted freelancers and vendors we bring in project by project.

Can you work on projects outside Chennai/Tamil Nadu?

Yes — we've worked on projects across India. For projects in other states we typically send our key creative people (director, cameraman) and pair them with vetted local crew and vendors in that geography.

Timeline & Cost
How long does a typical video take to produce?

Typically 2–6 weeks from the day the brief and concept are fully signed off, depending on format — around 2 weeks for an animated or live-action explainer, 4 weeks for a corporate film or ad film, and 3–4 weeks for a music video.

What's included — do you handle the whole process, or just the shoot?

End to end: concept and scripting, the shoot (crew, location, equipment), and post-production — edit, colour, sound and motion graphics. We also run ads on Meta, Google and Amazon, and facilitate commercial ad placements on satellite channels.

What does a project typically cost?

Depends on format, length, shoot days, locations and whether animation/VFX is involved — any number is a starting point, not a quote. As a rough guide: ad films typically start upward of ₹10 lakh, and corporate films (around an 8-minute runtime) usually run ₹8–12 lakh. Explainer videos and music videos depend on the specific brief. Share your brief and we'll give a clear, itemised estimate.
