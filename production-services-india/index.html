04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you provide local crew and equipment, or manage the whole shoot end-to-end?

Both — see Crew & Equipment if you need crew/gear support for a production you're running yourself, or Production Management if you want us to run logistics, permits and scheduling for you.

Can international productions shoot in India through you?

Yes — this is one of the core use cases for this service; we handle local crew, permits, locations and logistics for productions based elsewhere.

What permits/paperwork do you handle?

Everything needed for a hassle-free, professional shoot — location permissions, insurance, and any special approvals, depending on the project brief.

Do you provide equipment only, or equipment plus operators?

Some equipment comes with our own operators; for other cases, we coordinate with trusted vendors to get you the best pricing on equipment plus crew.
