03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you provide crew and equipment directly, or coordinate with local vendors?

We coordinate — sourcing and briefing local crew and equipment vendors around your production's specific requirements, rather than owning a fixed in-house kit list.

Can you support a production that already has its own crew from abroad?

Yes — this is a common setup. We fill the gaps around a visiting crew: local hires, equipment, locations, permits and day-to-day on-ground coordination.

How far in advance do you need dates and locations confirmed?

Earlier is better for equipment and crew availability, but share what you have — we'll tell you plainly if a date or location is tight once we check with local providers.

What regions in India can you support?

Tell us your locations and we'll confirm coverage and the right local partners for that specific region as part of the brief.
