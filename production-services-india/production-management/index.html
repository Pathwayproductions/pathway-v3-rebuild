03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

At what stage should production management get involved?

As early as creative pre-production, if possible — locations, budget and schedule decisions made early affect everything downstream, so earlier involvement usually means fewer surprises later.

Do you manage the budget, or just report on it?

Both — we manage spend against the agreed budget and provide clear, regular accounting and production reports so you always know where things stand.

Can you step in partway through a production that's already underway?

Yes — tell us where things stand and what's needed, and we'll scope what production management support makes sense from that point forward.

How does this differ from just hiring crew and equipment?

Crew and equipment coordination gets you the resources; production management is the ongoing planning, budgeting and communication layer that keeps a shoot on schedule and within scope. Many productions use both together.
