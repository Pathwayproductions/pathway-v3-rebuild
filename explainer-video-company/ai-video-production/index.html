04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What parts of the process actually use AI, and what's still done by your team?

Every stage — scripting, storyboarding, voice generation, the actual video or animation generation itself — uses specific AI tools. But it isn't a prompt-in, video-out process: a human is in the loop at every stage and every tool. “AI video” is really AI-assisted video, not fully automated video.

Who owns the rights to AI-generated content you produce for us?

You, as the client, own the rights — based on the usage-of-rights terms we agree with you at the start of the project.

What kind of projects is this best suited for?

Typically shorter-form, faster-turnaround explainers and social content rather than a flagship brand film — we'll advise if a project is better suited to traditional production instead.

When would you recommend AI video over traditional production, and is it actually cheaper or faster?

It's the right call for concepts that go beyond what's physically possible to film, or when budget/timeline genuinely won't stretch to a traditional shoot — and it is usually faster. It's not the right call when credibility matters and a real product, facility or process already exists to film instead. On cost: it's not guaranteed to be cheaper — it can sometimes end up costing more and taking longer than traditional production, so it's worth scoping properly rather than assuming it's the budget option.
