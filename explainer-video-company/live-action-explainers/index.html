05 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What's the difference between this and a regular corporate/ad film?

An explainer is built to teach — it has to make a concept or process clear and memorable, not just look good. The script and structure are built around clarity first.

Do we need our own location, or can you shoot on a set?

Either — many explainers work fine as studio/set shoots, which also keeps cost and schedule more predictable than a location shoot.

Can you combine live-action with graphics or on-screen text?

Yes — most live-action explainers include some motion graphics (labels, callouts, data visuals) layered in during post.

What do you need from us to start?

A clear sense of what you're explaining and who it's for — a formal script isn't required, we can build one from a conversation.

How do you decide whether the explainer should be presenter-led or shot as a real use-case scenario?

We work it out with you in the creative discussion — it depends on whether the product or process is easier to trust coming from a person speaking to camera, or from watching it actually being used.
