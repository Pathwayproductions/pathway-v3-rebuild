04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What kind of projects suit 2D animation over live-action?

Anything abstract, internal/technical, or where you'd otherwise need expensive sets, actors or locations to show — processes, software, data, concepts that don't exist physically.

Do you handle voiceover and sound design too?

Yes — voiceover casting/recording, sound design and music are part of the production, not a separate step you need to arrange.

How long does 2D animation typically take compared to live-action?

Roughly 2 weeks from an approved storyboard as a baseline — generally longer than a comparable live-action shoot, since every frame is built rather than captured. We'll give a realistic schedule once scope and style are set.

Can you match our existing brand's illustration style?

Yes, if you have an established visual/illustration style we design around it; otherwise we propose a style as part of the process.

How do you decide the story or character approach for a 2D animated explainer?

It's usually built around features, advantages and benefits, with typography doing a lot of the work alongside the visuals. Where a concept benefits from a character or a small narrative thread, we build the animation around that story instead.
