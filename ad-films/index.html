05 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What makes a good ad film different from a corporate film?

An ad film is built around a single idea, meant to be seen by an audience that doesn't already know you — it has to earn attention in the first few seconds. A corporate film assumes some existing interest and goes deeper.

Can you produce for TV, digital and social from one shoot?

Usually yes — we plan the shoot and edit to produce a master film plus cutdowns for different placements, rather than shooting each version separately.

Do you write the script, or do we bring one?

Either. Bring a brief or a rough idea and we develop the script and storyboard, or bring an approved script and we'll take it from there.

What's the one thing we should decide before briefing an ad film?

Specificity — the single message, feature or emotion you want a specific audience to take away, and where it will run. The platform (TV, YouTube, Instagram) genuinely changes the concept, format and media plan, so it needs to be part of the conversation from the start.

Is an ad film the right choice for our business?

Yes, if you have one specific message, feature or emotion to get across to an audience that doesn't already know you yet — ad films are built for exactly that single idea, in a brief, platform-driven format (TV, YouTube, Instagram).
