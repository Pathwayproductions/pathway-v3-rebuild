A Pathway Productions Film · The First Ever Crowd-Funded Tamil Film

Kuraiondrumillai.

A Tamil feature film about relationships and the challenges young people face in their personal and professional lives.

01 / The Story

Working Through the Differences.

He gives everything to the world and keeps nothing for himself. She keeps her heart locked away and her own life first. Falling for each other means learning why the other sees it so differently.

Krishna is a man who puts everyone else's needs before his own — imperfect, but never indifferent to the people and the society around him. Sandhya, a doctor, puts herself first, her own commitments ahead of everything else. When they meet, both are guarded, each afraid to let the other in without knowing what they'll get back. What neither expects is that it's their differences — not anything they have in common — that draws them together. Kuraiondrumillai follows Krishna and Sandhya as their contrasting characters collide, turning an unlikely romance into a window on two very different ways of relating to the world.

02 / How It Got Made

Made Possible by 60 Believers.

Kuraiondrumillai was raised entirely through crowdfunding — one of the first Tamil films made this way. Around 60 backers, many of them friends, funded the production directly, kept informed at every stage along the way. It was a new path for the industry at the time: a film built on belief, not a studio budget.
