04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What makes a lead “qualified” rather than just a lead?

A qualified lead has been checked against criteria you define — budget, intent, fit — rather than counted the moment someone fills a form. We agree the qualification criteria with you before a campaign starts.

Which channels do you use to generate leads?

Primarily Google, Meta and Amazon ads, plus AI-engine visibility where it's relevant.

Do you integrate with our CRM, or hand off leads manually?

We integrate directly with your CRM wherever possible, so leads flow straight in rather than being handed off manually — this is also what lets us close the loop on lead quality instead of just lead volume.

What happens to lead quality if we increase budget?

Not necessarily proportional — scaling spend can shift the balance between volume and quality, and we monitor for that rather than just chasing lower cost-per-lead.
