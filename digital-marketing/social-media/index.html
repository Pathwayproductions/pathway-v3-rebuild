03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you create the content, or manage/post content we provide?

We create it, as part of the service — the same creative team handles video, carousels, static posts and copy, and we build the content calendar around your products, goals and current trends.

Which platforms do you cover?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X (Twitter) are our main focus; we also manage other platforms on request, depending on what you need.

How do you measure success beyond likes and followers?

We track engagement quality and, where tracking allows, the link between content and actual leads/enquiries — not just vanity metrics like follower counts.

How often do you post, and who approves content before it goes live?

Posting frequency depends on the plan we agree with you, and the content calendar is shared around 15 days ahead. For approvals, we use Planable — every post goes up there with the creative, caption, hashtags and description, so your nominated approver can review and approve in one place.

Do you stick strictly to our brand guidelines for social content?

By default, yes — we follow the guidelines you give us. But where we see a real opportunity to reach a new audience, we'll sometimes suggest stepping outside them deliberately, as a proposal you approve rather than something we just do.
