04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you build on WordPress, a custom stack, or something else?

WordPress is our go-to platform for the front end.

Is SEO basics (speed, structure, metadata) handled as part of the build, or separate?

Handled as part of the build — a site that isn't structured for search from day one usually needs expensive rework later.

Do you handle ongoing maintenance/updates after launch, or only the build?

Yes — we can help with basic, necessary updates for up to 6 months after launch.

Can you migrate our existing content, or are we starting from scratch?

Either — we can migrate existing content and structure, or build fresh if you're rethinking the site entirely.
