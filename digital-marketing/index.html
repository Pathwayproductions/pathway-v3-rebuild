04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Getting Started
Do we need to use all your services, or can we start with just one?

Start with whichever is most urgent — SEO, PPC, social, or a specific channel. Many clients begin with one service and expand once it's working.

You do both digital marketing and creative production — is one more your main focus?

No — they're two dedicated teams, not one team doing double duty. The two also inform each other: digital marketing data shapes creative decisions, and creative insight shapes digital campaigns.

Can you run campaigns for a business outside Chennai/Tamil Nadu?

Yes — we manage digital marketing for clients across India; geography doesn't limit this the way it can for a physical shoot.

Scope & Team
Who actually manages our account — a dedicated person, or a team?

You get a single point of contact (SPOC) for client servicing, but the project itself is run by our internal team — even one social media post draws on several editors, graphic designers, copywriters and social media specialists working together.

How big is the team actually running our campaigns?

Digital Marketing is one of two dedicated teams within a focused in-house group of around 10–15 people — campaign strategists, a digital marketing manager, website developers and client servicing — backed by an extended network of specialists we bring in as needed.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Yes — 8 months.

Results & Reporting
How do you report on performance?

Through metrics and KPIs we agree with you upfront and tie back to your actual business goals — not just platform vanity numbers. You get a dashboard plus a monthly report built around those agreed KPIs.

How soon will we see results from digital marketing?

Not from month one — that's an expectation we'd rather correct upfront than let build frustration later. We set short-term goals for the first 6 months with a clear long-term vision beyond that.

Our goal isn't lead generation — can you still help?

Yes — the goal isn't leads for everyone. Depending on the client it could be awareness, personal branding, credibility or a wider social impact. We start by understanding what you're actually trying to achieve, then recommend the service mix that fits.

How We Work Together
How do you keep our leadership in the loop, not just our day-to-day marketing contact?

Through scheduled check-ins and a defined communication workflow between our team and yours, rather than updates only when something's wrong.

Do you need sign-off on every creative idea, or do you have room to experiment?

We agree clear expectations and boundaries with you upfront — what's fair game and what needs approval — and then use that room to actually experiment within it.

Do you wait for us to flag issues, or do you plan ahead?

We work with a digital marketing plan roughly a month ahead, so changes can be made proactively rather than reactively.

What happens before you actually start running campaigns?

Two audits, usually in parallel: first, your existing marketing tools and processes; second, your current campaigns, social presence, keywords, competitors and relevant trends. Both feed directly into the plan we bring back to you.

How often do we actually meet or hear from you?

Weekly to start, moving to roughly every 15 days once things settle, with a monthly report and review meeting as the fixed minimum either way.

Who from our side should be on the planning calls?

Ideally not just the marketing contact — sales, product, or anyone close to the customer, especially when the discussion is about lead quality or campaign direction.
