04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

How long before we see ranking improvements?

SEO is a medium-to-long-term channel — meaningful movement typically takes a few months, not weeks, and we'll set realistic expectations against your specific starting point and competition.

What does “AI Search” optimisation actually mean?

We work with you to choose the keywords that actually matter to your business, then track where you rank for them — not just on Google, but across AI answer engines and citations. For us, it means structuring and signalling your content so it's the kind of source these engines actually cite.

Do you do the content writing too, or just the technical/strategy side?

Yes, we do — though content reads best when it's written from your side, or with input and guidance from your team.

Will we get visibility into what you're actually doing each month?

Yes — transparency and regular updates are core to how we work with every client.

Do you also fix our website's SEO issues, or just the strategy?

We handle technical and on-page SEO as part of the service. If the website itself needs rebuilding or structural changes beyond that, we can do that too, but it's scoped and costed separately from the core SEO engagement.

Do you handle online reputation management (ORM) — responding to reviews, managing our reputation online?

Not as a standing service — ORM isn't something we offer. If something specific and urgent comes up, we can support on that case by case, but it isn't part of the regular SEO/AEO scope.
