02 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

How is this different from hiring individual freelancers per channel?

One coordinated programme and point of accountability across strategy, execution, optimisation and reporting, rather than separate people working on separate channels with no shared view.

How much input does our team need to provide day-to-day?

Regular check-ins and sign-off on strategy and spend, but day-to-day execution is managed on our side — the goal is to reduce your team's operational load, not add to it.

Which channels can be managed under this?

Depends on your brief — typically a combination of paid media, SEO, social and content, agreed and scoped upfront rather than fixed in advance.
