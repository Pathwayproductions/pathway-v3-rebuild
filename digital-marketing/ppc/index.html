04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Who owns the ad account — us or you?

You do — campaigns run on your own Google Ads/Meta accounts, so your data and history stay with you if we ever part ways.

How is budget decided, and who controls it?

We agree the KPIs and goals with you upfront, and your ad spend goes directly from you to Google or Meta — it never routes through us. We charge a separate management fee for running and optimising the campaigns.

How quickly can we expect leads/conversions to start coming in?

Paid search/social can start driving traffic almost immediately, but it usually takes some weeks of testing and optimisation before cost-per-lead stabilises.

Do you create the ad creative too, or just manage the campaigns?

We create it — a dedicated creative team sits inside our digital marketing service and produces videos, language adaptations, carousels, static posts and landing pages for the campaigns we run.
