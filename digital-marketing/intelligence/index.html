02 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

What data sources can be brought together?

Typically ad-platform data (Google, Meta), GA4, Search Console, organic social and CRM/lead data where it's connected — scope depends on what's available and what access is granted.

Is this a software product we'd log into?

Not yet — Pathway's own intelligence platform is still under development. Today this is a connected, analyst-led service: we bring the data together and report on it, rather than handing over a self-serve dashboard.

How often are reports and recommendations delivered?

Cadence is agreed as part of scope — commonly weekly or monthly, depending on how fast your channels move and how the team wants to receive updates.
