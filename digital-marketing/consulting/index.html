02 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Is this a one-off engagement or an ongoing relationship?

Either — a single strategy review for a specific decision, or ongoing periodic guidance alongside your team. Scope is agreed upfront.

Do you take over execution, or only advise?

Consulting here is advisory — your team retains ownership and execution. If you'd rather hand over execution too, that's Managed Marketing Services instead.

What do you need from us to give useful guidance?

Access to current performance data and an honest picture of what's working and what isn't — the more context we have, the more specific the guidance.
