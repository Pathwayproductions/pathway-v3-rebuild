04 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you manage our Amazon Seller/Vendor account, or only the ads within it?

Neither, directly — we're one of Amazon's authorised advertising agencies, so the ads themselves run through Amazon's own managed-service team. Pathway's role is to recommend strategy, keywords and campaign structure, and tweak campaigns as they run.

If Amazon's own team runs the ads, what does Pathway actually do?

We bring the strategy and the context Amazon's generic account team doesn't have — recommending keywords, targeting and campaign structure based on your specific products and goals, reviewing performance, and adjusting the plan over time.

How is this different from running Google/Meta ads for the same product?

Amazon ads target people already shopping with purchase intent on the platform, so strategy, keywords and creative all work differently from awareness-stage Google/Meta campaigns.
