03 / Common Questions

Before You Reach Out.

Do you help us choose which platform (Netflix, Amazon, regional OTT, etc.) to target?

For now we work with Amazon directly, and facilitate listing on YouTube Movies and Apple TV through associate vendors. We help you reach the right team at each platform — though final acceptance always depends on the platform's own review.

What technical specs/delivery requirements do you handle?

We provide a full spec sheet once the platform is confirmed — delivery requirements vary by platform.

How long does the OTT submission-to-listing process typically take?

Typically 4–8 weeks.

Do you also handle the marketing push once it's listed?

Yes, we do — see Film Marketing for the campaign side; this page covers getting the film onto the platform itself.
